# 🎨 Prompt Khusus: Wireframe Designer (Figma Plugin)

Semua instruksi di bawah ini telah diselaraskan **100% dengan tampilan visual asli web Anda** (berdasarkan *screenshot* desain: Header hijau tua, *background* abu-abu terang, sudut melengkung /*rounded corners*, dan desain tabel spesifik).

Gunakan plugin **Wireframe Designer** di Figma (pilih platform **Desktop/Web**), lalu *copy-paste* teks bahasa Inggris di bawah ini satu per satu:

### 💻 Prompt 1: Halaman Utama Kasir (POS Core)
> A modern POS (Point of Sale) cashier web interface with a light gray background. Top navigation bar is dark green with a white brand logo on the left and white menu links (Cashier, History, Report, FAQ) on the right.
> 
> The main layout is split into three vertical sections:
> 1. Left Sidebar (20% width): A vertical list of product categories with a white background.
> 2. Middle Content (50% width): A grid of product cards with rounded corners and subtle shadows. Each card has an image placeholder, product name, and price.
> 3. Right Sidebar (30% width): A fixed white shopping cart panel. Top section has a title 'Shopping Cart'. Middle section is a list of selected items showing item name, price, a text input for 'Notes', and quantity steppers (+/-). The bottom section has a gray top border and displays the 'Total Bill' and a large, rounded primary green button 'Process Payment'.

### 💻 Prompt 2: Modal Pembayaran (Payment Checkout)
> A payment confirmation modal dialog for a POS system. Clean, center-aligned card with rounded corners and a drop shadow, placed over a dark semi-transparent overlay. At the top, a dark green header with the title 'Payment Confirmation' in white text.
> 
> Below it, prominently display the 'Total Bill' in very large bold text. Below the total, provide a grid of two large selectable rectangular cards for payment methods: one for 'Cash' (with a green money bill icon) and one for 'QRIS/Digital' (with a blue QR code icon). 
> At the bottom of the modal, place a large primary green button labeled 'Complete Order'.

### 💻 Prompt 3: Halaman Riwayat Transaksi (Berdasarkan Screenshot)
> A transaction history web page with a light gray background. Top navigation bar is dark green with a white brand logo on the left and white text menu links on the right. 
> 
> Below the navigation, a page header section. The left side has a large title 'Riwayat Transaksi' and a small subtitle below it. The right side has a white, rounded rectangular filter box containing two date picker inputs ('Dari Tanggal' and 'Sampai Tanggal'), a green 'Filter' button with a funnel icon, and a light gray 'Reset' button. 
> 
> The main content area is a wide data table inside a white container with rounded corners and a subtle shadow. The table columns are: No. Order, Waktu, Metode Pembayaran, Total Belanja, and Aksi. 
> For the 'Metode Pembayaran' column, use pill-shaped badges: light blue background with blue text for 'Digital', and light green background with green text for 'Tunai'. The 'Aksi' column contains a white outline 'Detail' button with an eye icon for every row.

### 💻 Prompt 4: Modal Detail Transaksi
> A transaction detail modal dialog with rounded corners and a shadow, placed over a dark semi-transparent overlay. Top header with a dark background, title 'Transaction Detail', an order number string, and a close icon. 
> 
> The middle content area is a list of purchased items. Each item row displays the product name, quantity, item price, optional notes text (with a small yellow note icon), and the calculated subtotal. 
> 
> At the very bottom of the modal, a footer section with a light gray background showing the text 'Total Payment' on the left and the large total amount number in green text on the right.

### 💻 Prompt 5: Dashboard Laporan Keuangan
> A financial report web dashboard with a light gray background. Top navigation bar is dark green with a white brand logo and menu links. 
> 
> The page header has a title 'Laporan Keuangan' on the left and a white rounded filter box on the right with a date picker input and a green 'Tampilkan' button. 
> 
> Below the header, a grid of 4 summary metric cards with rounded corners: 'Total Pendapatan' (solid green background with a large faint wallet icon), 'Jumlah Transaksi' (white background with a blue icon), 'Pembayaran Tunai' (white background with an emerald icon), and 'Pembayaran Digital' (white background with an indigo icon). 
> 
> Below the summary cards, a white container with rounded corners titled 'Rincian Metode Pembayaran'. It contains a wide, stacked horizontal progress bar visualizing the percentage ratio between Cash (green) and Digital (blue) payments, with percentage labels on top.
